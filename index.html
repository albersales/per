About This App

Parañaque Emergency Ready (PER) was created by Zayon Systems for the people of Parañaque City — because in a crisis, every second counts. We believe that access to the right information at the right moment can mean the difference between life and death.

PER puts critical emergency contacts, your personal medical profile, and life-saving guides directly in your hands — no searching, no delays. This app is our commitment to the community: to keep families safer, to help first responders act faster, and to ensure that every resident of Parañaque City is Prepared, Educated, and Ready.
